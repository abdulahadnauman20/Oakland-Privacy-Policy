Oakland Diner Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Oakland Diner collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use the mobile application and related services.

Welcome to Oakland Diner (“Oakland Diner”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). By using the application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account or use our services, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password
  • Account authentication information

Order & Transaction Information

When placing orders through the app, we may collect:

  • Order details
  • Product selections
  • Cart contents
  • Delivery preferences
  • Table numbers for dine-in orders
  • Order notes or special requests
  • Reward or loyalty redemption information
  • Payment status and order history

Location Information

We may collect approximate or precise location data when:

  • Verifying dine-in eligibility
  • Supporting delivery functionality
  • Improving address selection accuracy

The app may also use Google Places services to help users search and select addresses.

Payment Information

Payments are processed securely through third-party payment providers such as Square, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

We do not store complete credit card numbers or payment card security codes on our systems.

Push Notification Information

If notifications are enabled, we may collect:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging device tokens
  • Device platform information
  • Notification interaction data

User-Generated Content

If available in your version of the app, users may upload or interact with content including:

  • Videos
  • Comments
  • Likes
  • Media content

Device & Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information including:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Network connectivity information
  • Crash or diagnostic information
  • Cached image and performance data

2How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users securely
  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Process payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Deliver notifications and updates
  • Enable location-based features
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

3How We Share Information

We may share information with trusted third-party providers necessary to operate our services, including:

  • Square for payment processing
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay for wallet payments
  • Google Places for address lookup services
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications

We may also disclose information:

  • To comply with applicable laws or legal requests
  • To protect our rights, users, or services
  • During a merger, acquisition, or business transfer

We do not sell users’ personal information to third parties.

4Payment Processing

Payments made through the app are processed by secure third-party payment providers.

Sensitive payment information such as complete card numbers is handled directly by payment processors and is not stored by Oakland Diner.

5Location Services

The app may request location permissions to support dine-in verification, delivery functionality, and address selection.

You can disable location access through your device settings at any time. Some app features may not function properly without location access.

Depending on available features, the app may request access to:
